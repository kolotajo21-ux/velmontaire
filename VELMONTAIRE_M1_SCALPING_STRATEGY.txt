VELMONTAIRE M1 IFVG-FVG IMMEDIATE SCALPING

SYMBOLS:
NAS100 and GER40.

TIMEFRAMES:
D1 and H4 are visual directional context only. Begin POI analysis on H1,
then refine it on M30 and M15. Execute entries on M1.

HTF CONTEXT:
Use either interaction with a direction-compatible H1, M30, or M15 Fair
Value Gap, or a sweep of significant fractal/session liquidity. A sweep of a
high provides bearish context. A sweep of a low provides bullish context.
D1/H4 context is informational and is not a separate algorithmic blocker.

TRADING WINDOW:
New entries are allowed only from 16:30 until 18:00 Europe/Kyiv. This is a
hard entry filter. A position opened before 18:00 remains open until Stop Loss
or Take Profit.

ENTRY MODEL:
Both LONG and SHORT entries are allowed. MARKET orders only.
After valid HTF context, an impulse M1 FVG must invert by a candle close beyond
its far boundary, creating an IFVG in the intended direction. An impulse FVG
has a direction-compatible middle candle whose real body is larger than the
real bodies of both neighbouring candles. Within the next three closed M1
candles, a new impulse FVG in the intended direction must form completely
inside the IFVG price boundaries. The IFVG and new FVG cannot be created by
the same candle. Enter MARKET immediately after the candle that completes the
new directional FVG closes; in causal bar simulation this fills at the next
M1 candle open. Do not wait for a retest, wick rejection, or engulfing
confirmation.

STOP LOSS:
Stop Loss is STRUCTURE. Place it beyond the nearest valid M1 invalidation zone
on the protective side of the entry: a confirmed swing low/high, a compatible
Order Block, or the IFVG. The new entry FVG is a trigger and is never itself a
Stop Loss reference. Use the nearest valid boundary and do not reduce the
structural invalidation distance.

TAKE PROFIT:
Risk reward is 1.0. Take Profit is exactly 1R from the actual entry and Stop
Loss distance.

FIRST PROBLEM AREA:
Before accepting the fixed 1R target, inspect the nearest opposing swing,
Order Block, FVG, or IFVG between the actual entry and Take Profit. It may be
traded through only when the stored H1/M30/M15 context direction matches the
trade and there is no fresh opposing H1/M30/M15 FVG or Order Block before the
1R target. Otherwise reject the trade because the required 1:1 is obstructed.

RISK:
Risk exactly 1.0% of account balance per trade using BALANCE_PERCENT sizing.
Maximum open positions: 2. Maximum open positions per symbol: 1. Maximum one
completed entry per Europe/Kyiv trading day per symbol. With NAS100 and GER40,
the planned combined daily risk is at most 2%.

Only when every required condition passes is a trade allowed.
