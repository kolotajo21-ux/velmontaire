VELMONTAIRE FX FVG REJECTION BLOCK IFVG-FVG

SYMBOLS:
AUDUSD and EURUSD.

TIMEFRAMES:
Execute entries on M15. Analyze directional order flow, FVG interaction,
and the Rejection Block on H4.

H4 DIRECTION AND CONTEXT:
Both LONG and SHORT entries are allowed. H4 order flow follows the latest
confirmed structural Break of Structure. LONG requires an H4 candle close
above a confirmed swing high; its latest confirmed protected swing low must
remain unbroken by a later H4 candle close. SHORT requires the exact mirror:
an H4 candle close below a confirmed swing low while the protected swing high
remains unbroken. Simultaneous HH plus HL or LH plus LL is not required.

Only a fresh direction-compatible H4 Fair Value Gap is valid. A bullish FVG
is the price gap between candle one high and candle three low. A bearish FVG
is the price gap between candle one low and candle three high. The middle
candle does not need a special body-size filter.

H4 FIRST TOUCH AND REJECTION BLOCK:
Only the first touch of the fresh H4 FVG is eligible. The first-touch H4
candle itself must be a direction-compatible Rejection Block. For a bullish
setup, its wick must enter the bullish FVG while both the candle open and
close remain above the FVG upper boundary. Its lower wick must be at least
2.0 times its real body and at least 50% of the full candle range. For a
bearish setup, apply the exact mirror using the upper wick and a candle body
below the bearish FVG lower boundary. If the first touch does not satisfy the
Rejection Block rule, that H4 FVG is consumed; later touches are invalid.

The valid H4 FVG plus Rejection Block context allows the M15 FVG inversion to
occur during the next 16 closed M15 candles, which is four hours. Only one
trade may be taken from one H4 FVG plus Rejection Block context.

M15 ENTRY MODEL:
After the valid H4 context, LONG requires a bearish M15 FVG to invert by an
M15 candle close above its far boundary. SHORT requires a bullish M15 FVG to
invert by an M15 candle close below its far boundary. This creates the IFVG.

Within the next 16 closed M15 candles after the inversion, a new standard
three-candle M15 FVG in the intended direction must form. The new FVG may
form anywhere; it does not need to be inside or overlap the IFVG. It must be
completed strictly after the inversion candle, so one candle cannot create
both stages. The valid inversion locks the setup, so this independent
four-hour post-inversion window may finish after the original H4 inversion
window. No retest or extra candle confirmation is required.

ENTRY:
MARKET orders only. Enter immediately after the candle completing the new
directional M15 FVG closes. In causal bar simulation, fill at the next M15
candle open. Trading is allowed around the clock with no session filter.

STOP LOSS:
Stop Loss is STRUCTURE. Place it beyond the farther protective boundary of
the M15 IFVG and the new M15 entry FVG, plus a 1 pip structural buffer. The
all-in risk calculation must also include spread, commission, and slippage.

TAKE PROFIT:
Risk reward is 2.0. Take Profit is exactly 2R from the actual MARKET entry
and final Stop Loss distance.

RISK:
Risk exactly 1.0% of account balance per trade using BALANCE_PERCENT sizing.
Maximum open positions: 2. Maximum open positions per symbol: 1. There is no
daily trade cap.

Only when every required condition passes is a trade allowed.
