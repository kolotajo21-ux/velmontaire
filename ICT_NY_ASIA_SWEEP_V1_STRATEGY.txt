ICT NEW YORK ASIA LIQUIDITY SWEEP M5 V1

SYMBOLS:
EURUSD, GBPUSD, XAUUSD and NAS100.

TIMEFRAMES:
Use only closed H1 candles for directional Bias. Execute the complete setup and
entry on M5. No other timeframe is used.

H1 BIAS:
Calculate EMA20 and EMA50 from closed H1 candle closes. LONG is allowed only
when the latest closed H1 candle closes above EMA20 and EMA20 is above EMA50.
SHORT is allowed only when the latest closed H1 candle closes below EMA20 and
EMA20 is below EMA50. Otherwise no new setup is allowed. Lock the Bias at the
close of the liquidity-sweep M5 candle.

ASIA LIQUIDITY:
Use only the current Europe/Kyiv trading day's Asia High and Asia Low. Build
the Asia range from closed M5 candles opening from 03:00 until 10:00
Europe/Kyiv. Other liquidity pools do not qualify.

NEW YORK WINDOW:
New setups, signals and LIMIT fills are allowed only from 09:30 until 11:00
America/New_York. The IANA timezone handles daylight-saving time causally. Any
unfilled order is cancelled at 11:00 America/New_York.

LIQUIDITY SWEEP:
LONG requires an M5 candle to trade below the Asia Low and close back above the
Asia Low while the locked H1 Bias is bullish. SHORT requires an M5 candle to
trade above the Asia High and close back below the Asia High while the locked
H1 Bias is bearish. A candle sweeping both Asia boundaries is ambiguous and
cannot start a setup.

M5 MARKET STRUCTURE SHIFT:
After the valid sweep, LONG requires an M5 candle close above the latest
causally confirmed M5 fractal swing high. SHORT requires an M5 candle close
below the latest causally confirmed M5 fractal swing low. A confirmed swing
uses exactly two candles on the left and two already-closed candles on the
right. The breaking close must cross the swing level from the opposite side.
Search the latest confirmed swing within the preceding 50 M5 candles.

DISPLACEMENT AND NEW FVG:
After MSS, the first qualifying direction-compatible standard three-candle M5
FVG owns the setup. A bullish FVG is the gap between candle one high and candle
three low. A bearish FVG is the gap between candle one low and candle three
high. The middle candle must be in the trade direction, must not precede the
MSS candle, must have a real body at least 1.0 times causal M5 ATR(14), and its
real body must be at least 60% of its full high-low range. FVGs completed before
the sweep are invalid for entry.

ENTRY:
LIMIT orders only. Place the order at exactly 50% of the first qualifying new
M5 FVG. The order becomes active on the next M5 candle after the FVG completes.
Do not chase price. Cancel an unfilled order at 11:00 America/New_York.

STOP LOSS:
Stop Loss is STRUCTURE. LONG places Stop Loss one minimum symbol tick below the
liquidity-sweep low. SHORT places Stop Loss one minimum symbol tick above the
liquidity-sweep high. The all-in risk calculation includes spread, commission
and slippage.

TAKE PROFIT:
Risk reward is exactly 2.0. Take Profit is exactly 2R from the actual LIMIT
entry and final Stop Loss distance.

RISK:
Risk exactly 0.50% of account balance per trade using BALANCE_PERCENT sizing.
Maximum open positions: 2. Maximum open positions per symbol: 1. Maximum one
completed entry per America/New_York trading session per symbol. Planned
combined open risk is at most 1.00%.

Only when every required condition passes is a trade allowed.
