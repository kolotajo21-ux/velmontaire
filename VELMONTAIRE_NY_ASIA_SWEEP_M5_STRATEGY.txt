VELMONTAIRE NEW YORK ASIA SWEEP M5 IFVG-FVG

SYMBOLS:
EURUSD, GBPUSD, NAS100 and SPX500.

TIMEFRAMES:
Execute and analyze the complete setup on M5.

LIQUIDITY SOURCE:
Use only the current Europe/Kyiv trading day's Asia High and Asia Low. Build
the Asia range from closed M5 candles opening from 03:00 until 10:00
Europe/Kyiv. Previous Day High/Low and other liquidity levels do not qualify.

NEW YORK ENTRY WINDOW:
New entries are allowed only from 09:30 until 11:00 America/New_York. This is
the first 90 minutes after the New York cash open and is a hard entry filter.
Using the IANA New York timezone keeps daylight-saving changes causal. A
position opened before 11:00 remains open until Stop Loss or Take Profit.

LIQUIDITY SWEEP:
Both LONG and SHORT entries are allowed. LONG requires an M5 candle to trade
below the Asia Low and close back above the Asia Low. SHORT requires an M5
candle to trade above the Asia High and close back below the Asia High. A
candle that sweeps both boundaries is ambiguous and cannot start a setup.

M5 ENTRY MODEL:
After the valid sweep and close back inside the Asia range, an opposing
standard three-candle M5 FVG must form and then invert by a candle close beyond
its far boundary. LONG requires a bearish FVG to invert by a close above its
upper boundary. SHORT requires a bullish FVG to invert by a close below its
lower boundary. The inversion must occur no later than 12 closed M5 candles
after the sweep.

Within the next 6 closed M5 candles after the inversion, a new standard
three-candle M5 FVG in the intended direction must form. It must complete
strictly after the inversion candle. Enter MARKET immediately after the candle
that completes the new directional FVG closes; causal simulation fills at the
next M5 candle open. Do not wait for a retest or additional confirmation.

STOP LOSS:
Stop Loss is STRUCTURE. For LONG, place it below the liquidity-sweep low. For
SHORT, place it above the liquidity-sweep high. Add a 1 pip structural buffer.
The all-in risk calculation must include spread, commission and slippage.

TAKE PROFIT:
Risk reward is 2.0. Take Profit is exactly 2R from the actual MARKET entry and
final Stop Loss distance.

RISK:
Risk exactly 0.35% of account balance per trade using BALANCE_PERCENT sizing.
Maximum open positions: 2. Maximum open positions per symbol: 1. Maximum one
completed entry per Europe/Kyiv trading day per symbol. Planned combined open
risk is at most 0.70%.

Only when every required condition passes is a trade allowed.
